Questions we hear often

What is an Employer of Record?

A company that legally employs someone on your behalf. The person works for you full-time, while the Employer of Record holds the employment contract and handles payroll, taxes, benefits and statutory filings in India.

Do we need an Indian company to work with you?

No. Foreign companies can hire through our Employer of Record service without an Indian entity. If you set one up later, we can transfer your team to it.

How quickly can someone start?

Mostly that depends on notice periods: many candidates in India serve 30 to 90 days with their current employer. Our side of the paperwork is ready before their first day.

What do the new labour codes change for us?

The four labour codes took effect in November 2025. Among other changes, PF and gratuity are now calculated on a wider definition of wages, fixed-term employees qualify for gratuity after one year, and registrations and returns are being consolidated. We review your salary structures and policies and tell you what needs to change.

Can you employ people in any Indian state?

Yes. Professional tax, labour welfare fund and establishment registrations differ by state, and we set these up as part of onboarding.

We already use payroll software. Why use you?

Software calculates. Someone still has to collect inputs, check them, file returns on time and answer employee questions. We do that work, on your software or ours.
