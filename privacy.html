Privacy notice

Last updated 15 November 2026

This notice explains what personal data Pavani IT collects through this website, why, and what you can ask us to do with it. It follows India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the rules made under it.

Who we are

Pavani IT provides recruitment, contract staffing, Employer of Record, payroll and compliance, training and HR advisory services in India. For this website, we decide why and how your personal data is used, which makes us the data fiduciary.

What we collect

When you send an enquiry, we receive your name, work email, company name, the service you’re interested in, approximate team size, and anything you write in the message box.

When you send us your CV, we receive what it contains, usually your contact details, work history, education and skills.

When you visit the site, we don’t use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies. Our fonts are served by Google Fonts, so Google receives your IP address and browser details when the page loads.

Please don’t send us sensitive information such as government ID numbers, bank details or health information through the website. If we need these later, for example to employ you, we’ll ask through a secure channel and explain why.

Why we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and discuss the services you asked about.
  • To prepare a proposal and, if you become a client, to provide our services.
  • To consider you for roles we are recruiting for, if you send us your CV.
  • To meet our legal obligations, for example keeping business records.

We use enquiry details because you shared them with us for that purpose. We use your CV with your consent, and we’ll ask you before sharing it with any client.

Who we share it with

  • Service providers that run parts of our business for us: our form delivery service, which passes website enquiries to our inbox, and our email provider. They may only use your data to provide that service.
  • Clients, only for candidates, and only after you agree to us putting you forward for a specific role.
  • Government authorities, when the law requires it.

We don’t sell your personal data or share it for advertising.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details for as long as we are in contact about your enquiry, and delete them within 12 months of our last contact unless you become a client. We keep CVs for up to 12 months so we can match you with new roles, unless you ask us to delete yours sooner. Where the law requires us to keep records for longer, we keep only what it requires.

How we protect it

We limit access to the people who need it, use providers that encrypt data in transit, and review who has access regularly. If a breach affects your data, we will tell you and the Data Protection Board of India as the law requires.

Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • tell you what personal data we hold about you and how we use it,
  • correct, complete or update it,
  • delete it, where we no longer need it for a legal reason,
  • withdraw your consent, for example to stop us considering your CV, and
  • nominate someone to exercise these rights for you if you die or become unable to.

Withdrawing consent doesn’t affect anything we did with your data before you withdrew it.

Questions and complaints

Email hello@pavaniit.com with “Privacy” in the subject line. We’ll confirm we received your request and respond within 30 days. If you aren’t satisfied with our answer, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

Changes to this notice

When we change how we handle personal data, we’ll update this page and the date at the top.
